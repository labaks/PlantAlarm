Plant Alarm is a simple watering reminder for your houseplants, with a free companion widget for Windows.

💧 Water-level indicator
See at a glance who needs watering: the droplet shows how much "water" is left until the next watering and gradually empties as the date approaches.

✅ Water any time
The "Water" button is always active — watering ahead of schedule just shows a quick confirmation instead of blocking the button.

📅 Flexible schedule
Every plant gets its own watering interval in days. You can set the last-watered date when adding a plant and adjust it later.

📷 Photos and notes
Add a photo and notes ("by the window", "doesn't like overwatering", fertilizer type) to keep track when you have a lot of plants.

🔔 Notifications
Your phone reminds you to water even when the app is closed — notifications are scheduled locally, no internet required.

🔄 Sync with desktop
There's a free Windows widget (github.com/labaks/PlantAlarm) — the phone and desktop sync your plant list directly over your local Wi-Fi network, no cloud or accounts involved.

💾 Backup
Export your plant list (with photos) to a file and restore it anytime — on your phone or on your computer.

No accounts, no sign-up, no internet required — all your plant data stays on your own device.
