Plant Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Plant Alarm is a Windows widget and Android app that remind you to water your plants. There are no servers, accounts, or sign-ups — this page explains what data is handled and how.

Your plant data

Each plant's name, watering interval, last-watered date, notes, and photo are stored locally: on desktop, in a file on your own computer; on mobile, in the app's local storage. This data is never sent anywhere and isn't visible to the developer — Plant Alarm simply has no server to send it to.

Syncing between devices

The desktop and mobile apps can sync your plant list directly with each other over your local Wi-Fi network (you enter the desktop's IP address manually in the app). Syncing never goes through the internet, the cloud, or any third-party server — only within your own home network.

Backup

The export/import feature creates a backup file only when you explicitly ask for one, and saves it wherever you choose (a folder on your computer or phone). The developer never receives a copy of this file.

Notifications

Watering reminders are scheduled locally on your own device and don't go through any third-party push notification server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The mobile app shows ad banners via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data, under its own Privacy Policy and Ads technology policy. You can manage ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads. As of this release only Google's official test ad units are in use — real ads will be enabled later, without changing what AdMob's own infrastructure collects.

Children

Plant Alarm isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Since everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app (and, on desktop, removing its data folder) fully deletes all of your plant data.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the app evolves — the current version is always available at this address.
